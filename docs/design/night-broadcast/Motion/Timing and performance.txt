NIGHT BROADCAST / TIMING AND PERFORMANCE

The exhibit arrives before the reaction. The audience gets a readable pause to inspect the decisive line. Treat these timings as starting points for animatics.

Supported / roughly 2.2 s
0.0 s: Read the exhibit. 0.5 s: Heave the approval stamp up. 1.0 s: Stamp the desk. 1.4 s: Bounce high enough for both feet and the robe to clear the bench. 2.2 s: Recover with an excessively formal bow. Settle on Supported and the cited finding.

Unresolved / roughly 2.6 s
0.0 s: Raise the gavel. 0.6 s: Notice the source detail. 1.2 s: Brake above the block. 1.8 s: Peer over crooked glasses. 2.6 s: Hold the result. On Mixed evidence, dart between exhibits. On Insufficient evidence, look for the missing detail. Display the specific label.

Contradicted / roughly 3.0 s
0.0 s: Inspect the source. 0.6 s: Tilt to read the decisive line. 1.2 s: Hold the deadpan stare. 1.8 s: Cut wide for the dive. 2.2 s: Put the ivory splat on the outer edge of the claim bubble. 3.0 s: Present the unchanged quote, formal verdict and source on stable ivory paper.

Use a quiet listening pose while the check is in progress. The silent version reads through pose, cut, expression and result label. The reduced-motion version presents a single illustrative still and goes straight to the finding and source. Keep repeated reactions short. Use the same performance for each speaker.
